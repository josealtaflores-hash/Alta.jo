ABOUT THIS BOOK

This is a collection of photographs from the places I’ve passed through, wandered into, and sometimes taken the long way around.

These images are about everyday movement—people going somewhere, waiting, working, walking, and simply existing in the spaces between destinations.

The book is a reminder that sometimes getting there matters less than noticing what’s around the corner.
